A FILM CAMPUS, NOT A COWORKING SPACE.

Everything gets better when the right people are in the same room.

Collective Studios is a national network built around the people who actually make films: cinematographers, producers, editors, colourists, film technicians, ADR engineers, sound mixers and finishing specialists.

Melbourne is the flagship. Byron Bay and the Gold Coast follow. The idea is simple: put exceptional craft under one roof and make the path from camera to screen radically easier.
